about=TerraAnalytica Work Log is a practical GIS and remote-sensing work diary, project time record, technical notebook and processing-provenance recorder for QGIS. Record project work by analyst, work class, duration and tags; search and filter accumulated records; and create compact CSV/PDF/print reports by project, user, class, tag and date. Reproducible workflows can import QGIS Processing History, retain algorithms, parameters and commands, record manual steps from SNAP, Python, R, Google Earth Engine, drone/photogrammetry software or fieldwork, store final parameters and inputs/outputs, and generate workflow diagrams and method records. An opt-in demo pack and built-in GIS/remote-sensing help make the plugin easy to evaluate. TerraAnalytica is the plugin brand, while exported reports can remain neutral or use a custom organisation heading. If Work Log is useful, users may choose to support ongoing development with a koha — a voluntary gift or contribution given in appreciation and reciprocity. Koha is optional and does not unlock features.
